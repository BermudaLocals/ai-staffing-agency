### Step 3.2: Best Practices Guide

**Share with client:**

```markdown
# AI Support Best Practices

## Optimizing AI Performance

### 1. Knowledge Base Quality
- Write clear, concise articles
- Use the same language your customers use
- Update articles based on common questions
- Remove outdated information promptly

### 2. Monitoring & Improvement
- Review AI conversations daily (first 2 weeks)
- Flag incorrect responses for retraining
- Add new articles for recurring questions
- Track resolution rate trends

### 3. Human Handoff Best Practices
- Respond to escalations within 5 minutes
- Read the full conversation history
- Don't ask customers to repeat information
- Thank them for their patience

### 4. Continuous Improvement
- Weekly: Review top unanswered questions
- Monthly: Analyze resolution rate by category
- Quarterly: Full knowledge base audit

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Setting and forgetting (no monitoring)
❌ Outdated knowledge base articles
❌ Slow human response after escalation
❌ Not using customer feedback
❌ Ignoring low-confidence responses
```

### Step 3.3: FAQ Document

**Provide client with internal FAQ:**

```markdown
# Internal FAQ: Using AI Support

## General Questions

**Q: How does the AI know what to say?**
A: The AI reads your knowledge base and uses that information to answer 
customer questions. The better your knowledge base, the better the AI.

**Q: What if the AI gives a wrong answer?**
A: Flag the conversation in the dashboard. We'll use it to improve the AI. 
You can also take over the conversation and correct the information.

**Q: Can customers tell they're talking to AI?**
A: We recommend transparency. The default greeting mentions AI assistance. 
Customers appreciate honesty and fast responses.

## Technical Questions

**Q: How do I add the widget to a new page?**
A: The widget automatically appears on all pages where the script is 
installed. No additional setup needed.

**Q: What if the widget doesn't appear?**
A: Check that the script is before the </body> tag. Clear your browser 
cache. Contact support if issues persist.

**Q: How do I update the widget appearance?**
A: Go to Settings > Widget > Appearance. Changes apply immediately.

## Operational Questions

**Q: How do I know when to take over a conversation?**
A: The AI will escalate automatically when:
- Customer requests a human
- Sentiment becomes negative
- AI confidence is low
- Conversation exceeds complexity threshold

**Q: What happens outside business hours?**
A: AI continues to respond 24/7. Escalations are queued for the next 
business day unless you have after-hours coverage.

**Q: How do I add a new team member?**
A: Go to Settings > Team > Add Member. Enter their email and role.
```

### Step 4.2: Testing Procedures

**Test Script:**

```markdown
# Pre-Launch Test Checklist

## Test 1: Basic Functionality
- [ ] Widget loads on homepage
- [ ] Widget loads on product pages
- [ ] Widget loads on mobile devices
- [ ] Widget opens when clicked
- [ ] Welcome message displays correctly

## Test 2: AI Responses
- [ ] Ask: "What are your business hours?" → Correct response
- [ ] Ask: "How do I reset my password?" → Correct response
- [ ] Ask: "What is your return policy?" → Correct response
- [ ] Ask: "asdfghjkl" → Graceful fallback
- [ ] Ask: "I want to speak to a human" → Escalation triggered

## Test 3: Escalation Flow
- [ ] Request human agent → Notification received
- [ ] Express frustration → Sentiment detection works
- [ ] Complex question → Low confidence escalation
- [ ] Agent can take over conversation
- [ ] Customer notified of human takeover

## Test 4: Integrations
- [ ] Slack notifications working
- [ ] Email notifications working
- [ ] CRM data syncing (if applicable)
- [ ] Order lookup working (if applicable)

## Test 5: Edge Cases
- [ ] Multiple browser tabs
- [ ] Page refresh during conversation
- [ ] Slow internet connection
- [ ] Browser back button
```

```markdown
# Quick Start Guide

## Logging In
1. Go to [LOGIN URL]
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"

- **Home:** Key metrics and recent activity

### When AI is Handling
- Conversations appear in "AI Active" tab
- Monitor for any issues
- AI will escalate when needed

### When You Need to Take Over
1. Click on the conversation
2. Click "Take Over"
3. Type your response
4. Click "Send"

### Closing Conversations
1. Ensure customer is satisfied
2. Click "Close Conversation"
3. Add tags if needed
4. Conversation moves to "Closed"

## Adding Knowledge Base Articles
1. Go to Knowledge Base
2. Click "Add Article"
3. Enter title and content
4. Select category
5. Click "Publish"

## Getting Help
- In-app chat: Click the help icon
- Email: support@[product].com
- Phone: [PHONE NUMBER]
```

## 📊 Success Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Definition | Target | Measurement |
|--------|------------|--------|-------------|
| **AI Resolution Rate** | % of conversations resolved by AI | 78%+ | Weekly |
| **First Response Time** | Time to first response | <5 seconds | Daily |
| **Human Response Time** | Time for human to respond after escalation | <5 minutes | Daily |
| **Customer Satisfaction** | Post-conversation rating | 4.5+/5 | Weekly |
| **Escalation Rate** | % of conversations escalated to humans | <25% | Weekly |
| **Knowledge Base Coverage** | % of questions with relevant articles | 90%+ | Monthly |

### Health Score Calculation

```
Health Score = (Resolution Rate × 0.3) + 
               (Satisfaction × 0.3) + 
               (Response Time Score × 0.2) + 
               (Engagement Score × 0.2)

Score Interpretation:
- 90-100: Excellent (Green)
- 70-89: Good (Yellow)
- Below 70: Needs Attention (Red)
```

### Early Warning Signs

| Warning Sign | Action |
|--------------|--------|
| Resolution rate <60% | Review knowledge base gaps |
| Satisfaction <4.0 | Analyze negative conversations |
| Escalation rate >40% | Check AI confidence settings |
| No logins in 3+ days | Reach out to check engagement |
| Support tickets increasing | Schedule troubleshooting call |

## 🎯 Onboarding Best Practices

### Do's

✅ **Personalize everything** - Use client's name, company, industry  
✅ **Set clear expectations** - Timeline, responsibilities, outcomes  
✅ **Celebrate milestones** - Acknowledge progress and wins  
✅ **Be proactive** - Reach out before they have to ask  
✅ **Document everything** - Notes, decisions, action items  
✅ **Follow up promptly** - Same-day responses to questions  

### Don'ts

❌ **Don't rush** - Quality setup prevents future problems  
❌ **Don't assume** - Verify understanding at each step  
❌ **Don't disappear** - Stay engaged throughout onboarding  
❌ **Don't skip training** - Untrained users = unhappy users  
❌ **Don't ignore red flags** - Address concerns immediately  
❌ **Don't over-promise** - Set realistic expectations
