## 📝 Recording Tips

### Before Recording
1. ✅ Clear browser cache and cookies
2. ✅ Close unnecessary tabs and applications
3. ✅ Disable all notifications (system, browser, phone)
4. ✅ Prepare demo account with realistic sample data
5. ✅ Practice the script 2-3 times
6. ✅ Test audio levels
7. ✅ Ensure good lighting if showing face

### During Recording
1. 🎯 Speak clearly and at moderate pace
2. 🎯 Pause briefly between sections (easier to edit)
3. 🎯 Move mouse smoothly and deliberately
4. 🎯 If you make a mistake, pause, then restart that section
5. 🎯 Keep energy consistent throughout
6. 🎯 Smile—it comes through in your voice!

### After Recording
1. 📁 Organize all footage by section
2. 📁 Back up raw footage immediately
3. 📁 Review for any errors before editing
4. 📁 Note timestamps for any retakes needed
