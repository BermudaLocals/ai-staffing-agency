## 🏢 General / Product Questions

### 1. What is AI Customer Support PWA?

**AI Customer Support PWA** is an intelligent customer support platform that uses advanced AI (powered by large language models like GPT-4 and Claude) to automatically handle customer inquiries 24/7. It integrates seamlessly with your website as a chat widget and can resolve up to 80% of customer questions without human intervention.

**Key capabilities:**
- 🤖 AI-powered chat that understands natural language
- 📚 Learns from your knowledge base and documentation
- 🔄 Seamlessly escalates to human agents when needed
- 📊 Comprehensive analytics and reporting
- 🔌 Integrates with your existing tools

---

### 2. How is this different from a regular chatbot?

Traditional chatbots are **rule-based** - they only respond to specific keywords or phrases and often frustrate customers with "I don't understand" messages.

Our platform uses **advanced AI (LLM technology)** that:
- Actually understands the meaning and context of questions
- Handles questions it's never seen before
- Maintains conversation context across multiple messages
- Detects customer sentiment and frustration
- Provides natural, human-like responses

**The difference:** Traditional chatbots resolve ~20-30% of inquiries. Our AI resolves 70-80%.

### 13. What counts as a "conversation"?

A **conversation** is a complete interaction between a customer and the AI/your team, from first message to resolution. 

**Counts as 1 conversation:**
- Customer asks a question, AI answers, customer leaves satisfied
- Customer asks multiple questions in one session
- Conversation that escalates to a human agent

**Does NOT count:**
- Widget loads but customer doesn't send a message
- Customer only views FAQ articles
- Internal testing by your team

## 🤖 AI & Features Questions

### 32. What AI technology powers the platform?

We use **state-of-the-art large language models (LLMs)** including:

- OpenAI GPT-4 and GPT-4 Turbo
- Anthropic Claude 3
- Custom fine-tuned models for specific use cases

Our AI layer automatically selects the best model for each query, optimizing for accuracy, speed, and cost.

---

### 33. How does the AI learn about my business?

**The AI learns from your knowledge base:**

1. You create articles with your FAQs, policies, and information
2. Our system processes and indexes this content
3. When customers ask questions, AI retrieves relevant information
4. AI generates natural responses based on your content

**Important:** The AI only uses YOUR content. It won't make up information or use generic responses.

---

### 34. What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

**We have multiple safeguards:**

1. **Confidence scoring:** AI indicates how confident it is
2. **Low confidence = escalation:** Uncertain responses go to humans
3. **Conversation review:** You can review all AI conversations
4. **Feedback loop:** Flag incorrect responses for improvement
5. **Continuous learning:** System improves based on feedback

**If a wrong answer slips through:**
- Customer can request human help anytime
- You can correct the response and update knowledge base
- AI learns from the correction

---

### 35. Can customers always reach a human?

**Absolutely!** Customers can reach humans through:

1. **Request:** Type "talk to human" or click the button
2. **Automatic escalation:** AI escalates when it can't help
3. **Sentiment detection:** Frustrated customers get escalated
4. **Business rules:** Certain topics always go to humans

You control the escalation rules and can make human access as easy or guided as you prefer.

---

### 36. How does sentiment detection work?

**Our AI analyzes customer messages for:**

- Frustration indicators (caps, punctuation, negative words)
- Urgency signals
- Confusion patterns
- Satisfaction indicators

**When negative sentiment is detected:**
- Conversation is flagged for attention
- Can automatically escalate to human
- Agents see sentiment score in dashboard
- Analytics track sentiment trends

---

### 37. Can the AI handle complex, multi-step questions?

**Yes!** Our AI maintains **conversation context**:

- Remembers previous messages in the conversation
- Handles follow-up questions naturally
- Can guide customers through multi-step processes
- Asks clarifying questions when needed

**Example:**
> Customer: "How do I return something?"
> AI: "I can help with returns! Is this for an online order or in-store purchase?"
> Customer: "Online"
> AI: "Got it. For online returns, here's what you need to do..."
