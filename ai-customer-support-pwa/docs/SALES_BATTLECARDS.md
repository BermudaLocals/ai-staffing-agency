## ❓ Discovery Questions

### Opening Questions

1. **"What prompted you to take this call today?"**
   - Reveals their pain point and urgency

2. **"Tell me about your current support setup."**
   - Understand their baseline

3. **"What's working well? What's not?"**
   - Identify specific problems to solve

### Pain Discovery

4. **"How many support tickets do you handle per month?"**
   - Quantify the opportunity

5. **"What's your average response time right now?"**
   - Benchmark for improvement

6. **"What percentage of tickets are repetitive questions?"**
   - Estimate AI resolution potential

7. **"How much are you spending on support today?"**
   - Calculate ROI potential

8. **"What happens when a customer waits too long for a response?"**
   - Uncover business impact

### Impact Questions

9. **"How does slow support affect your customer retention?"**
   - Connect to revenue impact

10. **"What would your team do with 80% fewer repetitive tickets?"**
    - Paint the positive future

11. **"If you could wave a magic wand, what would support look like?"**
    - Understand their ideal state

### Decision Process

12. **"Who else is involved in this decision?"**
    - Map the buying committee

13. **"What's your timeline for making a change?"**
    - Understand urgency

14. **"What would make this a no-brainer yes?"**
    - Identify key criteria

15. **"What concerns would you need addressed before moving forward?"**
    - Surface objections early

