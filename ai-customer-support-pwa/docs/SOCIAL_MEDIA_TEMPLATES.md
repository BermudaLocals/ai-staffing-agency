## 💡 Engagement Tips

### Responding to Comments

1. **Respond within 1 hour** - Algorithm rewards quick engagement
2. **Ask follow-up questions** - Keep the conversation going
3. **Use their name** - Personal touch matters
4. **Add value** - Don't just say "thanks!"
5. **End with a question** - Encourage continued engagement

### Example Responses

**Generic Comment:** "Great post!"
**Bad Response:** "Thanks!"
**Good Response:** "Thanks, [Name]! What's your biggest support challenge right now? Would love to hear your perspective."

**Question Comment:** "How much does this cost?"
**Bad Response:** "Check our website."
**Good Response:** "Great question! Plans start at $297/mo with a 14-day free trial. Want me to DM you more details?"

**Negative Comment:** "AI support is terrible."
**Bad Response:** [Ignore or delete]
**Good Response:** "I hear you—bad AI IS terrible. That's exactly why we built something different. What was your worst experience? We'd love to show you how modern AI is different."

### Engagement Tactics

1. **Post at optimal times** (see calendar above)
2. **Engage with others first** - Comment on 10 posts before posting
3. **Use Stories/Reels** - Algorithm favors video content
4. **Go Live occasionally** - Highest engagement format
5. **Collaborate** - Tag partners, customers, influencers
6. **Repurpose content** - Turn posts into carousels, threads, videos
7. **User-generated content** - Share customer posts and testimonials

## 📊 Analytics Tracking Guide

### Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| **Reach** | People who saw your content | +10% monthly |
| **Engagement Rate** | (Likes+Comments+Shares)/Reach | 3-6% |
| **Click-Through Rate** | Clicks to website/Impressions | 1-3% |
| **Follower Growth** | New followers per period | +5% monthly |
| **Share Rate** | Shares/Total engagement | 10%+ |
| **Save Rate** | Saves/Reach (Instagram) | 2-5% |
| **Conversion Rate** | Signups from social | Track via UTM |

### UTM Parameter Template

```
https://yoursite.com/?utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=[campaign]&utm_content=[post-type]
```

**Example:**
```
https://yoursite.com/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=jan2026&utm_content=educational-post
```
